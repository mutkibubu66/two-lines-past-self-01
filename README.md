# Two Lines to Your Past Self

[![make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca)](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca)

## The two lines

> Don't send that 2 a.m. email to your boss — sleep on it, because the version you write angry will be the version they remember.
>
> That one unsent draft taught me that a night of waiting costs nothing and a hasty sentence costs a reputation, so now I never send anything I wrote after midnight.

Line 1 warns my past self about one specific moment: the 2 a.m. email
to the boss, written angry. Line 2 traces what changed because of that
moment — one unsent draft became a rule: nothing written after midnight
ever gets sent.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (two lines, under 400
characters total, valid UTF-8) and prints them to stdout. Pure standard
library, no network.
